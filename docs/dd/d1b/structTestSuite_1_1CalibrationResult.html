std::string TestSuite::CalibrationResult::status

"ok", "no_load" (the test has no load knob), "over_budget" (fails even at a load of 1), "hit_cap" (still passes at the largest load searched), or "unconfirmed" (the search found a passing load but the confirmation runs failed, even after stepping the load down), or "excluded" (the test opted out of calibration).
